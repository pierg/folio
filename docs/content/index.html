folio turns a coding agent into the librarian of a knowledge library that lives inside a project, or on its own. Every document has one genre, which fixes its job, its voice, its format and its checks. Documents link to each other, and maps give a reader the way in. You talk to your agent; it follows folio's seven skills and runs the gate before every commit.
