An essay published in The Atlantic Monthly in July 1945. It argues that the record of research has outgrown the means of consulting it, and describes the memex, a device that stores a person's records and joins items into associative trails.

It is read online in The Atlantic's archive, at theatlantic.com. That address was not looked up when this source was filed, so it is not listed as a verified identifier.
