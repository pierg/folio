When a skill, a genre card or a page disagrees with the specifications, the specifications win, and the other is fixed.

The specifications are the contract. The model comes first: every other spec builds on it and settles one part in detail. They are Markdown files in the repository, outside this library, so their rows link to GitHub.

Come here with a question, not to read front to back. The table below says which file to open.

Which file to open

Find the question you have on the left. The file on the right settles it.

  • What a document, a link, a map, the journal or the charter is, and what the gate promises.model.md
  • How to write or read a genre card: its settings, fields, parts and sections.genre-format.md
  • Why the gate stopped a commit, what a check looks at, and how severe it is by default.checks.md
  • What a folio command does, and which files it changes.commands.md
  • How a pack bundles genres, rules and journal kinds, and how a workflow is written.pack-format.md
  • The shape every skill follows: how it starts, its rules, its steps, where it stops.skill-format.md
  • What the shell draws around a page, and which components and tokens a page may use.COMPONENTS.md
