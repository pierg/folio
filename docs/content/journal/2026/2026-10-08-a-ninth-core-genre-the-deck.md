A library that gives talks from its knowledge had nowhere to keep the slides: a designed entry could hold them, but nothing presented them. The deck genre holds a talk as a div of slides, each drawn at 1600 by 900 and scaled to its frame, with the speaker's notes beside each. The page shows every slide complete, for reading and comments; Present shows one at a time, full screen, stepping through builds; a presenter's window shows the next slide, the notes and a timer, and the two stay in step. Print gives one slide per page. The card requires the deck and its slides and a citation, since a talk rests on the library's documents.
