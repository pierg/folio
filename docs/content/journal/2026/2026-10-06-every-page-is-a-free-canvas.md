Genre cards now say what a document is for and how it speaks, never how it looks. Page genres lost their word caps and the rules that forced headings and sections; journal entries, results and claims keep their caps. The shell gives every HTML page the whole width as a canvas with its own style and script, and a page asks for the narrow reading column only with the layout meta. Math renders with KaTeX when a page asks for it. The pages of this library were redesigned to show the standard.
