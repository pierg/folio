folio 0.3.0 gives the genres a placement on the maps. Concepts, notes, entries, guides, decks, readings and surveys are placed: each is one row on one home map, under a heading, and the new placement check names any that is on none, on two, or outside every heading. Sources are cited: they take no row, and a map page lists the sources its rows cite. The catalog records each place, the shell draws it as a path above the title, and the rail reads the same data. A new review mode, save.review after, lands pages live and lists them on the Review page until the owner marks them reviewed. In this library the essay's source left the Using folio map's rows; the map's prose links it instead.
