---
title: Paper versions are snapshots
description: A paper's source stays revised, and each frozen version is a permanent copy beside it.
genre: journal
date: 2026-10-03
kind: decision
about: [folio, permanent-record]
tags: [papers]
---

A frozen paper could never be revised, so the model changed: main.tex is revised, and freezing copies the source and the built PDF into a permanent versions/<name>/ folder. A later change is frozen as a new version. See [Permanent record](/content/concepts/permanent-record/).
